What is Internet Technology?

Internet technology refers to the systems and tools that allow computers and devices to communicate with each other through a global network. It enables people to share information, send messages, access websites, and perform many online activities.

Modern internet technology connects billions of devices worldwide and plays an essential role in education, business, communication, and entertainment.
